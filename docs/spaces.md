# Spaces

A space is a separate workspace with its own bots, chats, memory and computer, such as one for work
and one for home.

## Create a space

Choose **Create** (the **+** at the top of the sidebar), then **New private space**. You switch
to it from the sidebar, and each space has its own bots, groups and sections.

Most people only need one. Add a space when work must stay apart: a client, a side project, or
personal life.

## What stays inside a space

Nothing crosses between spaces unless you allow it:

- Chats, files and [memory](./memory.md).
- The Team Computer and every bot's [computer](./computer.md).
- [App connections](./integrations.md).
- [Saved credentials](./bot-secrets.md), when the space keeps its own.
- Skills a bot saves.

Bots only talk to bots in the same space, unless you turn on **Allow cross-space bot communication**
in a bot's settings.

## What follows you everywhere

Your account settings apply in every space: [models](./models.md), [voice](./voice-and-calls.md),
[approvals](./approvals.md) and your credentials vault. Skills, MCP servers and plugins you add are
available in all your spaces too, unless you narrow them with **Available in**.

## Customize one space

Right-click a space in the sidebar and choose **Settings**, or open **Settings → General** and choose
**Manage** next to the space.

There you can rename the space, or give it its own **Models**, **Voice**, **Approvals** or **Vault**
with **Customize**. **Use defaults** goes back to your account settings.

**Delete space** removes the space and its bots. This can't be undone.
