# Meetings

Send a Rakazo bot to Zoom, Google Meet, or a Microsoft Teams work meeting. After the call, you can ask the bot what was discussed and download a speaker-labeled transcript.

## Send a bot to a meeting

1. Open a direct chat with the bot. On web or desktop, select **Meetings** in the chat header.
   On mobile, tap the bot's name in the chat header to open its profile, then tap **Meetings**.
2. Get permission to record and share the transcript with the Space. Paste the attendee link into **Meeting link** and select **Join meeting**.
3. Admit the bot from the waiting room and grant capture permission if the meeting platform prompts you.

On Rakazo's hosted service, participants see the Rakazo logo and the bot's name on its camera tile. The bot leaves automatically after everyone else leaves. Select **Leave meeting** in Rakazo to remove it sooner.

## Ask about the call and find the transcript

When the call ends, the bot can acknowledge it in chat while it waits for the transcript. Once the transcript is ready, ask the bot in the same chat. For example, ask “What did we decide?” or “Who is following up on the demo?”

Everyone in the Space can open the shared text file in **Artifacts**, filtered by the bot. The person who sent the bot can also open **Meetings** → **View transcript** or delete the original transcript and its file there. To publish an older private transcript, open it and select **Share transcript with Space**.

## Schedule a bot from your calendar

If **Auto-join** is available under **Meetings**, connect Google Calendar and select a meeting or its recurring series. Admit the bot if the meeting platform prompts you.

For your own deployment, see [Meeting bot setup](./self-host-meetings.md).
